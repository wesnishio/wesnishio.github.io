Wes Nishio

Founder of Palkie Talkie, a voice AI that gets you speaking a second language the way you speak your first.

I grew up in Osaka, where a guy who isn't funny barely exists and "nice guy" is short for "guy who doesn't matter". In Japanese I live by how I say a thing. In English, for years, I was the nice guy.

Palkie Talkie is the coach I wanted. It is curious about you, so it keeps asking and you keep talking, and along the way it hands you the words for the things you tell it.

Before Palkie Talkie I built GitAuto, an AI agent that writes and passes unit tests for legacy code, Q, an AI chat inside Slack, and WorkStats, an engineering metrics dashboard. Before that I spent six years as a software engineer, and before code, investment banking at Barclays in Tokyo. Master's in Electrical Engineering from Kyoto University. I live in San Francisco.
